What is ChainSight?

ChainSight is a supply chain simulation platform that quantifies the systemic impact of operating choices on service, cash, and cost. It covers 6 core modules: Order & Shipment Generation, MRP, Production Planning, Deployment, Truck Planning, and Execution.

It simulates end-to-end supply chain operations, enabling business to quickly test What-if scenarios to optimize decisions across the entire value chain.

AI ChainSight is built on top of the existing ChainSight Simulation Core Engine, aiming to further elevate its capabilities through Ontology, Causal DAG, and LLM technologies.

The Business Problem

Scenario construction, data preparation, simulation, and insight generation for an E2E cross-node supply network requires in-depth business expertise and technical mastery.

The path from OS to parameter to business metrics is not deterministic — the real supply chain system and operation model evolves with time and business context.

The Goal

An AI ChainSight that is self-aware and capable of autonomous self-iteration. It learns from interaction, detects its own knowledge gaps, and autonomously evolves its ontology, causal model, and reasoning capabilities — moving from tool to thinking partner.

Eventually, it grows to be an integrated OS expert.
