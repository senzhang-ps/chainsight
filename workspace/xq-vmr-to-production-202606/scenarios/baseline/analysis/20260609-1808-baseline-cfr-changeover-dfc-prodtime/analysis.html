Background & Context

This is the baseline reference run for project xq-vmr-to-production-202606: the current-state simulation of the HC/PCC flow at the XQ plant (1864) from VMR to production, covering 2026-06-29 to 2026-11-01. It establishes the comparison point for later what-if scenarios on AO ratio and advance lead time. The project's focus metrics are MOE, national inventory and service; this report drills into three operational views requested on top of that baseline:

  1. CFR (case fill rate / service) by category × production line × month
  2. Changeover count by production line × month × changeover id
  3. Changeover cost by production line × month × changeover id
  4. Month-end DFC (days forward coverage) by category × production line × month
  5. Production total time (production + changeover, hr) by category × production line × month

Analysis Scope

  • Simulation window: 2026-06-29 → 2026-11-01 (June and November are partial months).
  • Network: XQ plant 1864; customer demand is booked at downstream DC locations and attributed back to the producing line.
  • Category: HC (category_en = Hair) and PCC. Source: Fresh pull from Databricks ps_psc_sku_master (category_en). Databricks coverage 236/286; after line-inference for new VMR materials, 278/286. By order qty: databricks 83.8%, line-inferred 14.0%, unmapped 2.1%.
  • Production line: each material mapped to its delegate_line from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg (material level). Unmapped materials: 80853439, 80881660, 83908390.
  • Month-end DFC: DC ending inventory (22 DC locations, plant 1864 excluded) divided by average daily demand-forecast over the next 30 days. Forecast horizon ends 2026-11-08, so months 2026-10, 2026-11 have a shortened forward window (flagged with *) and are read with caution.
  • Production total time: production time (constrained planned qty ÷ production rate, unit/hr) plus changeover time, in hours, by month (2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10, from production_plan_date). All production is at plant 1864; no production is booked in June/November.
  • Scenario identity: output filtered by run_id = db_baseline_20260606_205517; config by config_name = baseline.

Key Findings

  • Overall baseline CFR is 93.6%. Category split — Hair: 97.2%; PCC: 88.7%; Unmapped: 99.1%;
  • Line Unmapped has the lowest service (22.7%); month 2026-11 is the weakest period (23.9%).
  • Month-end DC coverage averages 32.5 days; lowest-coverage lines are XQHD (17.9d); XQHK (20.3d); XQHG (21.1d); Read service (CFR) and coverage (DFC) together: thin DFC on a low-CFR line points to a true supply gap.
  • Changeover cost concentrates on: XQHK (945,770); XQHH (593,810); XQHV (458,430);
  • The dominant cost driver is the high-cost changeover id(s); review the count×unit-cost mix per line for reduction levers.
  • Production total time is 7,852 hr across 4 producing months, of which 21.1% (1,653 hr) is changeover. Busiest line XQHK (2,179 hr); busiest month 2026-07 (2,573 hr). Lines with the highest changeover share of line time: XQHK (28%); XQHJ (27%); XQHD (19%);

Recommendations

  • Use this baseline strictly as the reference for the upcoming AO-ratio and advance-lead-time what-ifs; compare the same four views to isolate service vs. inventory vs. changeover trade-offs.
  • Investigate line Unmapped and month 2026-11 for the service gap (capacity, changeover load, or supply timing); cross-check its month-end DFC to tell a demand miss from a stock-out.
  • Where DFC runs high while CFR is healthy, there is inventory-reduction headroom; where DFC is thin (<1–2 weeks), protect service before pushing AO ratio up.
  • Target changeover-cost reduction on the top cost lines via sequencing/family-batching to cut high-cost changeover ids.

Appendix — Definitions & Calculation Notes

  • CFR = Σ shipment_qty / Σ order_qty (ratio of sums; numerator and denominator aggregated separately to each month × category × line, then divided). Order log (module1_output_orderlog) is deduplicated on the 7-field natural key (simulation_date, date, material, location, demand_type, advance_days, quantity) before summing; shipment log (module1_output_shipmentlog) needs no dedup. Month = business date as YYYY-MM.
  • Production line = delegate_line from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg, joined on material (each material has exactly one delegate line). Demand at all DC locations is attributed to the producing line. Unmapped materials: 80853439, 80881660, 83908390.
  • Changeover count & cost = Σ count / Σ cost from module4_output_changeoverlog, grouped by month × line × changeover_type. The changeover_type column equals the changeover_id in cfg_m4_changeoverdefinition.
  • Month-end DFC (days forward coverage) = month-end DC ending_soh (from module5_output_stockonhandlog, summed over the 22 DC locations, plant 1864 excluded) ÷ average daily forward demand. Forward demand uses the weekly demand forecast (cfg_m1_demandforecast) at DC locations, spread to a daily rate and summed over the next 30 days (clamped to the forecast horizon 2026-11-08). DFC is a ratio of sums at each rollup (Σ inventory ÷ Σ daily-demand-rate), so groups are demand-weighted. Month-end snapshot dates: 2026-06 = 2026-06-30; 2026-07 = 2026-07-31; 2026-08 = 2026-08-31; 2026-09 = 2026-09-30; 2026-10 = 2026-10-31; 2026-11 = 2026-11-01. Months with a shortened window: 2026-10, 2026-11. Sim day 1 = 2026-06-29 (forecast week 1).
  • Category = category_en from Databricks cdl_ps_hana_prd.sl.ps_psc_sku_master (material_num → category_en). Fresh pull from Databricks ps_psc_sku_master (category_en). New VMR materials absent from the master inherit the category of their delegate_line when that line is single-category among mapped materials (flagged category_source = line-inferred in extracts/category_resolution.csv); mixed lines stay Unmapped. Resolution by order qty: databricks 83.8%, line-inferred 14.0%, unmapped 2.1%. Materials still without a category: 21184776, 21184777, 21200191, 21205101, 21387644, 21403972, 21420779, 21425608.
  • Production total time (hr) = production time + changeover time. Production time = con_planned_qty (constrained planned production, from module4_output_productionplan) ÷ prd_rate (production rate, unit/hr, from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg), computed per material then summed. Changeover time = module4_output_changeoverlog.time (already = changeover id × count × per-event changeover time, hr), summed per line. Month = production_plan_date (the day the line runs); production spans 2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10 at plant 1864. Changeover is line-level and is allocated to category pro-rata by production hours within line × month (only the mixed line XQHD is split). Extracts: prodtime_*.csv.
  • Scope filters: run_id = db_baseline_20260606_205517; config_name = baseline.
