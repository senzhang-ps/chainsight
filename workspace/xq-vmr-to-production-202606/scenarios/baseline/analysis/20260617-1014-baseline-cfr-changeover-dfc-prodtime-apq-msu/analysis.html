Background & Context

This is the baseline reference run for project xq-vmr-to-production-202606: the current-state simulation of the HC/PCC flow at the XQ plant (1864) from VMR to production, covering 2026-06-29 to 2026-11-01. It establishes the comparison point for later what-if scenarios on AO ratio and advance lead time. The project's focus metrics are MOE, national inventory and service; this report drills into three operational views requested on top of that baseline:

  1. CFR (case fill rate / service) by category × production line × month
  2. Changeover count by production line × month × changeover id
  3. Changeover cost by production line × month × changeover id
  4. Month-end DFC (days forward coverage) by category × production line × month
  5. Production total time (production + changeover, hr) by category × production line × month
  6. APQ (avg constrained planned qty per production run) by production line and by material
  7. Wash count / MSU by production line (wash = changeover id 2/3; MSU = qty × SU factor ÷ 1000)

Analysis Scope

  • Simulation window: 2026-06-29 → 2026-11-01 (June and November are partial months).
  • Network: XQ plant 1864; customer demand is booked at downstream DC locations and attributed back to the producing line.
  • Category: HC (category_en = Hair) and PCC. Source: manual-input filled 44 categories Databricks coverage 280/384; after line-inference for new VMR materials, 384/384. By order qty: databricks 60.0%, line-inferred 40.0%, unmapped 0.0%.
  • Production line: each material mapped to its delegate_line from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg (material level). Unmapped materials: 21359277, 21368921, 21377834, 21479013, 21479014, 21483803, 21488493.
  • Month-end DFC: DC ending inventory (22 DC locations, plant 1864 excluded) divided by average daily demand-forecast over the next 30 days. Forecast horizon ends 2027-01-03, so months none have a shortened forward window (flagged with *) and are read with caution.
  • Production total time: production time (constrained planned qty ÷ production rate, unit/hr) plus changeover time, in hours, by month (2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10, from production_plan_date). All production is at plant 1864; no production is booked in June/November.
  • APQ: total constrained planned qty ÷ number of production runs, where a run is a module4_output_productionplan row that begins with a changeover (changeover_id not null); reported by production line and by material.
  • Wash count / MSU: wash count = changeover-id-2, 3 event count per line; MSU = Σ (constrained qty × SU factor ÷ 1000) over the line's materials. SU factor from the local SUF workbook (primary; 191 materials) with Databricks su_factor_for_buom fallback (102) and manual input (89).
  • Scenario identity: output filtered by run_id = db_baseline_20260616_154430; config by config_name = baseline.

Scope note. APQ averages the constrained planned quantity over the number of production runs. The denominator counts plan rows whose changeover_id is non-null (each such row is a run that begins with a changeover); the 5,256 continuation rows with a null changeover id are excluded so a single run is not double-counted. A higher APQ means larger, less-frequent batches; a lower APQ means more, smaller runs (more setups per unit). Full per-material detail is in extracts/apq_by_material.csv.

Key Findings

  • Overall baseline CFR is 84.9%. Category split — Hair: 95.7%; PCC: 79.0%;
  • Line Unmapped has the lowest service (0.0%); month 2026-06 is the weakest period (40.4%).
  • Month-end DC coverage averages 28.5 days; lowest-coverage lines are XQHK (20.4d); XQHG (22.8d); XQHJ (25.6d); Read service (CFR) and coverage (DFC) together: thin DFC on a low-CFR line points to a true supply gap.
  • Changeover cost concentrates on: XQHK (952,378); XQHD (744,040); XQHG (573,244);
  • The dominant cost driver is the high-cost changeover id(s); review the count×unit-cost mix per line for reduction levers.
  • Production total time is 14,320 hr across 4 producing months, of which 15.2% (2,170 hr) is changeover. Busiest line XQHG (2,636 hr); busiest month 2026-08 (4,420 hr). Lines with the highest changeover share of line time: XQHK (28%); XQHA (16%); XQHJ (16%);
  • APQ averages 1,748 units/run. Largest batches: XQ Line 10 (3,151); XQHG (2,262); XQHV (2,002); smallest: XQHK (1,170); XQHA (1,121); Lower APQ lines run more, smaller batches (more setups per unit).
  • Wash intensity is 0.326 washes/MSU overall (4,308 wash changeovers / 13,218.5 MSU). Most wash-intensive: XQHH (0.697); XQHA (0.684); XQHJ (0.593); these lines change formula most often relative to volume — prime targets for sequencing/family-batching.

Recommendations

  • Use this baseline strictly as the reference for the upcoming AO-ratio and advance-lead-time what-ifs; compare the same four views to isolate service vs. inventory vs. changeover trade-offs.
  • Investigate line Unmapped and month 2026-06 for the service gap (capacity, changeover load, or supply timing); cross-check its month-end DFC to tell a demand miss from a stock-out.
  • Where DFC runs high while CFR is healthy, there is inventory-reduction headroom; where DFC is thin (<1–2 weeks), protect service before pushing AO ratio up.
  • Target changeover-cost reduction on the top cost lines via sequencing/family-batching to cut high-cost changeover ids.

Appendix — Definitions & Calculation Notes

  • CFR = Σ shipment_qty / Σ order_qty (ratio of sums; numerator and denominator aggregated separately to each month × category × line, then divided). Order log (module1_output_orderlog) is deduplicated on the 7-field natural key (simulation_date, date, material, location, demand_type, advance_days, quantity) before summing; shipment log (module1_output_shipmentlog) needs no dedup. Month = business date as YYYY-MM.
  • Production line = delegate_line from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg, joined on material (each material has exactly one delegate line). Demand at all DC locations is attributed to the producing line. Unmapped materials: 21359277, 21368921, 21377834, 21479013, 21479014, 21483803, 21488493.
  • Changeover count & cost = Σ count / Σ cost from module4_output_changeoverlog, grouped by month × line × changeover_type. The changeover_type column equals the changeover_id in cfg_m4_changeoverdefinition.
  • Month-end DFC (days forward coverage) = month-end DC ending_soh (from module5_output_stockonhandlog, summed over the 22 DC locations, plant 1864 excluded) ÷ average daily forward demand. Forward demand uses the weekly demand forecast (cfg_m1_demandforecast) at DC locations, spread to a daily rate and summed over the next 30 days (clamped to the forecast horizon 2027-01-03). DFC is a ratio of sums at each rollup (Σ inventory ÷ Σ daily-demand-rate), so groups are demand-weighted. Month-end snapshot dates: 2026-06 = 2026-06-30; 2026-07 = 2026-07-31; 2026-08 = 2026-08-31; 2026-09 = 2026-09-30; 2026-10 = 2026-10-31. Months with a shortened window: none. Sim day 1 = 2026-06-29 (forecast week 1).
  • Category = category_en from Databricks cdl_ps_hana_prd.sl.ps_psc_sku_master (material_num → category_en). manual-input filled 44 categories New VMR materials absent from the master inherit the category of their delegate_line when that line is single-category among mapped materials (flagged category_source = line-inferred in extracts/category_resolution.csv); mixed lines stay Unmapped. Resolution by order qty: databricks 60.0%, line-inferred 40.0%, unmapped 0.0%. Materials still without a category: none.
  • Production total time (hr) = production time + changeover time. Production time = con_planned_qty (constrained planned production, from module4_output_productionplan) ÷ prd_rate (production rate, unit/hr, from cfg_m4_materiallocationlinecfg), computed per material then summed. Changeover time = module4_output_changeoverlog.time (already = changeover id × count × per-event changeover time, hr), summed per line. Month = production_plan_date (the day the line runs); production spans 2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10 at plant 1864. Changeover is line-level and is allocated to category pro-rata by production hours within line × month (only the mixed line XQHD, XQHG is split). Extracts: prodtime_*.csv.
  • APQ (avg constrained planned qty per production run) = Σ con_planned_qty ÷ production-run count. A production run = a module4_output_productionplan row whose changeover_id is non-null (the row begins with a changeover); continuation rows with a null changeover id are not counted as separate runs. Reported overall, by production line, and by material (each material over its own run count). Run basis: non-null changeover_id plan row (= a changeover starts the run); qty basis: con_planned_qty (constrained planned = produced here). Extracts: apq_by_line.csv, apq_by_material.csv.
  • Wash count / MSU = wash count ÷ MSU per line. Wash count = changeover events with id in 2, 3 from module4_output_changeoverlog. MSU = Σ (con_planned_qty × SU factor ÷ 1000) over the line's materials. SU factor source: SUF workbook workspace\xq-vmr-to-production-202606\scenarios\baseline\config\SUF for XQ HC ChainSight.xlsx (primary, 191 materials) with Databricks su_factor_for_buom fallback (102 materials) and manual input (89 materials); 0 missing. Overlapping sources agree to ~0.05%. Extracts: wash_msu_by_line.csv, msu_by_material.csv.
  • Scope filters: run_id = db_baseline_20260616_154430; config_name = baseline.

Extract CSVs backing every table are in extracts/; the consolidated workbook is the companion .xlsx.
